# Artwork provenance

ASCEND's cover and companion atlas were generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project on October 8, 2026. They use original characters and no Solo Leveling or Pokemon assets. The original generated files were copied into project assets and encoded as WebP without changing the illustration.

## Cover prompt

A premium wide 2:1 DEV article cover and adventure-game hero illustration for the original app ASCEND. An explorer in a dark jacket and backpack looks toward a glowing turquoise circular gate framing trees and sunrise, with a small original leaf-eared fox companion. Anime-inspired urban fantasy, deep ink navy and emerald green, mint light and warm orange dawn. Quiet dark space at left, exact text ASCEND and THE WORLD IS YOUR QUEST. No copyrighted characters, logos, fake screenshots, or watermarks.

## Companion atlas prompt

One transparent three-column, two-row atlas of six separate original full-body fantasy companions: a leaf-eared fox, moss-covered stone tortoise, luminous mint moth, midnight blue owl, amber salamander, and white fawn with star-shaped antlers. Consistent anime game illustration, expressive faces, transparent padding, no labels, panels, or copyrighted characters.
